# PN Junction Diode: Working, Mechanism and Applications

## 1. Introduction

A PN junction diode is formed by joining a p-type semiconductor and an n-type semiconductor. It is the most basic semiconductor device and is widely used in electronic circuits.

---

## 2. Formation of PN Junction

When p-type and n-type materials are joined:

* Electrons from n-region diffuse into p-region
* Holes from p-region diffuse into n-region

This diffusion leads to recombination near the junction.

As a result:

* A region without free charge carriers is formed
* This region is called the depletion region

---

## 3. Depletion Region

The depletion region contains:

* Immobile positive ions (n-side)
* Immobile negative ions (p-side)

This creates an internal electric field.

This electric field opposes further diffusion of charge carriers.

---

## 4. Potential Barrier

Due to the electric field, a potential barrier is formed.

Typical values:

* Silicon → ~0.7 V
* Germanium → ~0.3 V

This barrier prevents the flow of majority carriers under normal conditions.

---

## 5. Biasing of PN Junction

### Forward Bias

Condition:

* p-side connected to positive terminal
* n-side connected to negative terminal

Effects:

* Depletion region width decreases
* Barrier potential reduces
* Current flows easily

Result:
→ Large current flows

---

### Reverse Bias

Condition:

* p-side connected to negative terminal
* n-side connected to positive terminal

Effects:

* Depletion region width increases
* Barrier potential increases
* Very small current flows

Result:
→ Almost no current (only leakage current)

---

## 6. V-I Characteristics

Forward bias:

* Current increases exponentially after threshold voltage

Reverse bias:

* Very small constant current
* Sudden increase at breakdown

---

## 7. Breakdown Mechanism

At high reverse voltage:

Two types:

### Zener Breakdown

* Occurs at low voltage
* Due to strong electric field

### Avalanche Breakdown

* Occurs at high voltage
* Due to collision of charge carriers

---

## 8. Applications of PN Junction Diode

* Rectifiers (AC to DC conversion)
* Signal detection
* Voltage regulation
* Switching circuits
* Clippers and clampers

---

## 9. Summary

A PN junction diode is formed by joining p-type and n-type semiconductors.
It allows current in one direction and blocks in the other.
Forward bias allows conduction, reverse bias blocks it.
It is the basic building block of electronic devices.
